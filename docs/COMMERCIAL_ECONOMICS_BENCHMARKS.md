# DialDash Commercial Economics Benchmarks

## Purpose

These benchmarks help estimate what an energy creator's real transaction may be worth when the prospect does not publish a price. They are sales-planning comparables, not claims about personal income or a prospect's undisclosed rate.

DialDash applies evidence in this order:

1. `VERIFIED`: the prospect's own public price or a disclosed transaction involving that prospect.
2. `ESTIMATED`: a public filing, government rate card, or first-party industry price for a comparable transaction.
3. `INFERRED`: the revenue engine follows from the role or category, but the offer or price is not yet verified.

Every revenue-stack item receives its own economics. Research, advisory, board compensation, fund economics, and speaking are not blended into one number.

Each benchmark also distinguishes:

- **Named person to study:** a real operator whose role and business model illustrate the avatar.
- **Price publisher:** the prospect's company, a buyer's public filing, or a comparable public rate card.
- **Connection to price:** whether the number is that person's own public offer, an observed firm transaction, or only a market estimate.

Named examples include Rory Johnston and Emmet Penney for paid research; Arjun Murti for advisory, investing, and board roles; Mark LaCour for energy sponsorship and speaking; Eric Woodroof for training and expert projects; Olivier Corradi, John Sodergreen, and Het Shah for energy data products; and David Hunt for cleantech executive search.

## Interpretation Rules

- A board seat is not a creator offer. It is an authority-linked economic layer and should not be pitched as if content directly sells appointments.
- A fund's fee model is not the partner's income. Assets, fee offsets, expenses, carry realization, and ownership share are usually unknown.
- Public-company board comparables should not be applied to private or advisory boards without a large discount and explicit uncertainty.
- Institutional or regulatory content may create stakeholder value without a creator-controlled customer transaction. In that case DialDash shows no invented customer price.
- The outreach conversation should connect content to the actual commercial outcome: qualified subscribers, mandates, product trials, sponsor inventory, enrolment, executive mindshare, or deal relationships.
